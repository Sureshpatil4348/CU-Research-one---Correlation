Why this dig

Partial / residual correlation v001 already strips a leave-currency-out USD strength factor from all eight majors and reports one full-sample rho_partial per pair. This folder asks a different question on a narrower universe: among the seven USD majors only, after the USD factor is removed, does the leftover link still move at the same pace as raw Pearson correlation through time?

  • Universe: EURUSD, GBPUSD, AUDUSD, NZDUSD, USDJPY, USDCHF, USDCAD — 21 unordered pairs.
  • Left out: EURGBP (not a studied leg; never a USD-factor constituent).
  • Two views: full-sample partial vs Pearson (level / gap), and rolling raw vs in-window partial on W ∈ {20, 60, 100} (path and pace).

Experiment setup

  • Snapshot: 2026-11-03_majors_alpari_D1 under data/snapshots/2026-11-03_majors_alpari_D1.
  • Bar filter: Dropped unfinished last bar dated 2026-11-03 UTC after a tick-volume check against the prior complete day.
  • Series: daily log return of close, ln(closet / closet−1). Quote direction unchanged; no inversion.
  • Control z: equal-weight mean of USD-strength returns from majors that do not share a non-USD currency with either leg. USD-strength is minus the log return when USD is the quote and plus the log return when USD is the base.
  • Full-sample: OLS of each leg on z with an intercept; rho_partial is Pearson of the residuals. Same-sample raw Pearson r and the gap rho_partial − r are side checks. Fisher 95% intervals are shown; p-values stay secondary.
  • Rolling partial: inside each window W, rho_partial(W) = (rxy − rxz ryz) / sqrt((1 − rxz²)(1 − ryz²)). This is the correlation of the in-window OLS residuals.
  • Pace metrics: path agreement = correlation of the raw and partial rolling series; pace agreement = correlation of their first differences; sign agreement = share of days with the same sign; mean gap and mean absolute gap.
  • Headline window: W = 60. Windows 20 and 100 stay in the tables and charts.
  • Runner: run_seven.py. Figures live under outputs/figures/.

How to read

  • Full-sample gap — level shift after USD removal. A large |gap| does not by itself say the rolling path lost its tempo.
  • Path agreement — whether the residual correlation series tracks the same ups and downs as raw rolling r.
  • Pace agreement — whether day-to-day changes of the two series move together. This is the main “respects the pace” score. Headline window is W = 60.
  • Sign agreement — fraction of days where raw and partial share the same sign. Useful when a level shift flips the band.
  • W = 20 / 60 / 100 — short / medium / longer pace. Short windows are noisier; longer windows are slower.
  • Scores are symmetric in the two symbols. They do not prove cause, lead-lag, or a hedge.

Conclusion

What we got to know. Removing the USD factor changes more than the level. On W=60, path and pace agreement with raw rolling Pearson are often weak, so the residual tempo can diverge from the raw correlation path. Across the 21 pairs, mean W=60 path agreement is 0.644 and mean W=60 pace agreement is 0.661. 1 pairs sit at pace agreement ≥ 0.85; 16 sit below 0.70. Median absolute full-sample gap is 0.298.

Where the level moves most. Largest |rho_partial − r| gaps: AUDUSD-USDCHF (gap=0.5994, rho_partial=0.2191, r=-0.3803); EURUSD-NZDUSD (gap=-0.5869, rho_partial=-0.0359, r=0.5510); EURUSD-AUDUSD (gap=-0.5731, rho_partial=-0.0159, r=0.5572). Those pairs show that a strong raw Pearson can be largely a shared USD move; the residual link can sit near zero or even flip sign. That is a level story.

Where the pace holds or breaks. Highest W=60 pace agreement: EURUSD-USDCHF (0.8608). Lowest W=60 pace agreement: EURUSD-AUDUSD (0.5477). Strongest full-sample residual link: AUDUSD-NZDUSD (rho_partial=0.7351). Weakest (most negative): EURUSD-USDCHF (rho_partial=-0.6198).

Practical takeaway for this research stream. Use full-sample partial to judge how much of a major–major Pearson link is USD. Use the W=60 pace agreement to judge whether the residual series still moves with the same tempo as raw rolling correlation. When pace agreement is high, stripping USD mainly re-levels the path. When it is low, the residual tempo itself diverges and needs a pair-by-pair read of the three-panel chart. This page is a reading of these 21 pairs only. It is not a trading signal.
