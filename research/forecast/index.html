Frozen protocol (summary)

Full text lives in v001/PROTOCOL.md. score.py stores its SHA-256 and refuses to rescore if the file changes after scores exist.

Targets

T1 spike when next-20-day mean turbulence exceeds the causal 90th percentile after 252 completed windows.

T2 break when |forward 20-day Pearson − expanding Pearson| > 0.30.

Estimator

Univariate logistic on one standardized feature. First prediction after 1,000 labeled days. Refit every 21 days. Training rows embargoed by 20 trading days so label windows end before the test block.

Inference

Brier loss. DM with Newey–West lag 20. BH at 0.05 on the nine competitors. Hansen MCS, block 20, 400 draws, seed 20261103, 90% set.
